Our Story

Born From Fire,
Made With Love

PizzaFire started in a tiny kitchen in 2019 with one obsession: making the perfect pizza. Founder Musab Khan spent years perfecting his hand-stretched dough recipe, importing San Marzano tomatoes, and sourcing the finest mozzarella.

Today, we serve hundreds of pies a day — but every single one still feels personal. Because when you eat here, you're not just having pizza. You're having our pizza.
